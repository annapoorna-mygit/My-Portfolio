Project Overview

ThermoRL is a reinforcement-learning scheduler that co-optimizes compute placement across heterogeneous cooling zones (air-cooled, direct-to-chip liquid, and single-phase immersion) by jointly minimizing carbon emissions and thermal stress. It introduces the Thermal Headroom Index (THI), a unified, zone-agnostic metric, and formulates scheduling as a Constrained Markov Decision Process solved via Lagrangian-relaxed Proximal Policy Optimization.

Impact: Simulation on a digital-twin environment modeling a 50 MW AI campus demonstrates 73% reduction in thermal limit violations, 18% cooling energy savings, and 12% carbon reduction versus carbon-aware-only baselines, without degrading job completion time.

Problem Statement

AI training workloads impose extreme, dynamic thermal loads on data center infrastructure. A single NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack dissipates 120 kW with pulsed thermal transients at 10–100ms timescales during synchronized gradient updates. Current carbon-aware schedulers optimize placement based on grid carbon intensity but assume infinite cooling headroom. When cooling capacity is exceeded, emergency responses (fan ramp-up, chiller boost) consume 30–40% more energy, negating carbon savings.

  • Immersion tanks: High thermal inertia but face dielectric fluid degradation above 50°C
  • Direct-to-chip cold plates: Rapid response but vulnerable to pump cavitation under fluctuating loads
  • Air-cooled zones: Cannot absorb sudden density spikes without exceeding ASHRAE limits (27°C)

Solution Architecture

ThermoRL introduces a closed-loop scheduling architecture with three core components:

  • Thermal Headroom Index (THI): Unified, zone-agnostic scalar combining temperature, heat-load ratio, and degradation state. THI = 1 means full headroom; THI → 0 means approaching thermal limits.
  • Lagrangian PPO Agent: Constrained MDP with 4 actions (assign, migrate, throttle, defer). Reward co-optimizes carbon, cooling energy, and throughput. THI ≥ 0.1 enforced via dual gradient ascent.
  • Digital Twin Simulator: Gymnasium-compatible environment modeling 3 cooling zones with calibrated thermal RC dynamics. Power profiles from Alibaba GPU traces; carbon signals from electricityMap.
